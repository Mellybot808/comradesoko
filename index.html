SAFETY, PRIVACY & FAIR DEALING

Community policies.

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW

Important: These are proposed launch policies, not a legal agreement. ComradeSoko is currently a browser-only prototype. It does not verify people, process payments, hold or release funds, collect identity documents, operate subscriptions, or provide a staffed 24-hour dispute service. Have a Kenyan lawyer and appropriate financial-regulatory specialists review any escrow service before launch.

Who we are, how the marketplace works & community rules

Who we are

ComradeSoko — “Soko la Comrades na Majirani” — is intended to connect students, nearby businesses, and campus communities around Kenyan institutions. Every member can browse and order from others, and can also list their own goods or services. People participate as a buyer or seller depending on each transaction; neither is an exclusive account role. We introduce community members; we do not own or sell listed goods.

Joining is free in this prototype. Proposed future listing limits and Local Provider subscriptions are not active, and no fees or subscriptions are collected here.

Direct transactions

Members arrange each order directly with one another, currently through WhatsApp. The same person may buy in one transaction and sell in another. ComradeSoko does not currently handle goods, delivery, payments, or escrow. M-Pesa Direct and cash on delivery are arrangements between the parties; M-Pesa transaction charges are the person paying unless agreed otherwise. Any future escrow fees are unapproved proposals requiring separate terms. Marketplace listings and contact links are demonstration data in this prototype.

Our proposed terms do not replace rights or responsibilities that apply under Kenyan law. Any limitation of liability is subject to applicable law and legal review; please resolve delivery timing, quality, availability, and payment details directly with the seller before ordering.

Eligibility and prohibited listings

Proposed launch rules: users must be at least 18, use their real name and phone number, and post genuine photos. Do not list drugs, weapons, stolen goods, exam leakages, adult content, or other unlawful or harmful items. The prototype cannot verify age, identity, phone ownership, listing ownership, or whether a report is true.

Two verified scam reports are intended to trigger a permanent ban and phone-number blacklist after a fair review. Reporting, verification, banning, and blacklisting are not active in this prototype. Fake or abusive reviews may be removed through a future moderation process. Reviews cannot be deleted by their author under the proposed policy; applicable legal rights and moderation decisions still apply.

These proposed terms are intended to be governed by the laws of Kenya, with Kisumu or Kisii courts as the proposed venue, subject to legal review and mandatory venue rules.

Proposed escrow terms & delivery confirmation (draft — not active)

Not a live service: ComradeSoko does not accept deposits, hold money, release payments, or provide escrow today. The dashboard preview is stored in one browser and records simulated confirmations only. Never send money to ComradeSoko based on the demo.

1. Parties and transaction agreement

A future escrow transaction would require the buyer and seller to agree in writing to the item or service, condition or scope, total price, delivery method and location, delivery deadline, any delivery charges, and the payment and dispute steps before funding. Both parties would need verified accounts and access to the same transaction record. Transactions involving prohibited or unlawful goods or services would not be eligible.

2. Funding and safeguarding

If introduced, funds would be collected and safeguarded only through an approved payment provider and a legally compliant arrangement. ComradeSoko staff must not use personal M-Pesa numbers or ordinary business accounts as an informal holding account. Funding would count only after the payment provider confirms it; screenshots or a buyer's “sent” message alone are not proof. The buyer should never share an M-Pesa PIN or one-time code.

3. Delivery, acceptance and proposed release

After delivery or completion, the buyer would inspect the item or service and confirm receipt in the transaction record; the seller would separately confirm delivery or completion. A future payment provider—not a browser button or an individual admin—would release funds only under the published payout rules after both parties confirm, or after a documented review resolves a dispute under those rules. The exact confirmation window, acceptable evidence, partial-delivery handling, cancellation and refund rules must be agreed and published before launch.

4. Disputes and admin review

Either party should be able to flag non-delivery, a material mismatch, damage, an incorrect amount, or suspected fraud before payout. A disputed payout would be paused with the payment provider while an authorized reviewer examines relevant order details and evidence, gives both parties a fair chance to respond, records the decision and reason, and provides an appeal or escalation route. Admins must not change payment status without an auditable server-side record. Response times, evidence retention, privacy safeguards, appeals, and urgent safety handling still require definition.

5. Fees, risk and legal review

Earlier planning mentioned possible fees of 2% for Comrade Sellers and 4% for Local Providers; these are unapproved proposals, are not charged, and would need confirmation, clear disclosure of who pays, tax treatment, and agreement before a transaction begins. Any final escrow terms must explain provider charges, refunds, failed or reversed payments, payout timing, responsibilities, limitations, complaint handling, and applicable consumer rights. Obtain Kenyan legal and financial-regulatory advice and confirm an appropriate licensed payment-provider arrangement before offering this service.

Privacy policy

Prototype data and proposed collection

Listings, reviews, comments on products, campus posts and room listings, inbox messages, simulated escrow records, and other marketplace demo activity are stored in this browser and are not shared with other users. You can create a browser-local profile without verification. If you choose to sign in when Firebase is configured, authentication details are sent to Firebase Authentication, an optional profile photo is uploaded to private Firebase Storage, and profile details are saved in a user-restricted Firestore record. Avoid entering payment details or identity documents.

Firebase Authentication verifies email through a secure sign-in link, phone through an SMS code, or Google sign-in. These checks verify access to a contact method, not legal identity. Identity documents are not collected by this system; any future collection requires a separate, reviewed verification process.

Use, access and deletion

In a future service, profile and shop information would be used to show relevant listings and facilitate order alerts. Provider identity documents would be restricted to authorized administrators and never shown to buyers; buyers would see the business name and contact phone. We do not intend to sell personal data or collect M-Pesa PINs.

Server-side account deletion within seven days after a WhatsApp request is a proposed service commitment, not an available prototype feature. For this local demo, use “Clear local profile” in the dashboard and clear this site’s browser data to remove locally stored prototype information. Support: WhatsApp “Delete my account”.

Seller policy: Comrade Sellers & Jirani Providers

Comrade Sellers (students)

  • Must be a student at the institution where they sell and may be asked to provide a student ID after secure verification is available.
  • Use real product photos, mark stock out when sold, and deliver as promised.
  • The proposed free account limit is 20 products. The prototype does not securely enforce account limits.

Local Providers / Jirani Soko (town businesses)

  • Business or delivery service must be within 5 km of each campus served. Location is not verified by this prototype.
  • Future verification is planned to require a shop-front photo, owner ID, and M-Pesa Till. Do not send ID documents through this prototype; secure verification is not available yet.
  • Keep prices fair and within 15% of the local town average, with no exam-season price hikes. State delivery fees and times clearly, for example “KES 100 to Manyatta, 45 mins,” and keep open/closed status current.
  • Proposed pricing: 30 days free, then KES 199/month Starter for up to 3 institutions or KES 499/month Pro for all institutions and homepage featuring. Subscriptions, plans, and featured placement are not active.
  • Three confirmed delivery failures are intended to trigger a downgrade after review. That process is not active in this prototype.
Ordering, delivery tips & support (for everyone)
  • For delivery orders, order only when you have money and are at your hostel or agreed delivery location.
  • Check gas or water before the rider leaves. If you are absent, pay the rider’s agreed return fare.
  • Leave an honest review and report suspected scams to WhatsApp support with relevant screenshots and payment reference. Do not post sensitive payment or identity details publicly.

Support and dispute mediation within 24 hours are planned service targets, not guaranteed or currently staffed service levels. Contact support Monday–Saturday, 8 AM–7 PM, for the proposed operating hours.

Local Provider agreement (proposed, for nearby businesses)

By registering as a Jirani Provider, I confirm that my business is legal and within 5 km of the schools I serve; my prices are fair to comrades; and I will deliver on time or communicate delays. I understand the proposed 30-day free period and subscription fees required to remain verified after that period.

I understand that shop photos may be used by ComradeSoko for TikTok marketing only under the proposed provider agreement. Two verified scam reports may result in a permanent ban and phone-number blacklist after review. Verification, subscriptions, marketing use, reporting, and digital signatures are not active in this browser prototype.

Digital signature: a required checkbox is shown in the local-provider profile form as a prototype acknowledgement only. This browser cannot securely capture or retain a legally reliable signature.
